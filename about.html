The person behind Product Moat

Hi, I'm Martin.

I've always been a little obsessed with one question: how did you actually end up in product management?

For me, the honest answer is - I got lucky. I was practically pushed into the role before I even realized I'd already been doing the job for a while. No master plan, no five-year strategy. Just a good nudge at the right moment.

That accidental start turned into a genuine love for the craft, and more than 12 years building product across different industries, countries, and company types - mostly SaaS. Every environment taught me something different about what "good" product work actually looks like, and how differently people get there.

Product Moat is for anyone who shares that same curiosity: people who want to hear how others really found their way into product, how they think, and how the craft changes shape depending on where you practice it. If that sounds like you, you're in the right place.
