Privacy Policy

This page explains what information ProductMoat collects, why, and how it's handled — whether you're just browsing, applying to be featured, or recommending someone else.

Last updated: August 26, 2026

1. Who we are

ProductMoat (productmoat.com) is an interview series run by Martin Knapic. This policy covers the information collected through the website — browsing the directory and map, applying to be featured, recommending someone else, and signing in with LinkedIn to verify an application.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the site, we collect:

  • Application details — name, role, company, location, LinkedIn URL, personal website, X/Twitter handle, years of experience, a short bio, a pull quote, an optional photo, and any Q&A answers you write when applying to be featured.
  • Recommendation details — the LinkedIn URL of the person you're recommending, your own name and email if you provide them, and any note you add about why they'd be a good fit.
  • LinkedIn sign-in data — when you sign in with LinkedIn to verify an application, we receive your name, email, and profile photo from LinkedIn's OpenID Connect response. This is used only to confirm you are who you say you are before unlocking the application form.
  • Local preferences — your light/dark theme choice, stored in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it on your next visit.

3. How we use it

We use the information above to:

  • Review applications and recommendations, and decide who to feature in the series.
  • Verify that an application is genuinely coming from the person it claims to be from, via LinkedIn sign-in.
  • Publish an interview — name, role, company, links, bio, photo, and Q&A — on productmoat.com and share it across our distribution channels (LinkedIn, the newsletter, X, Facebook), if and when you're featured, and always with your knowledge.
  • Remember your theme preference between visits.

We don't use your information for anything beyond running and improving ProductMoat.

4. LinkedIn sign-in

Signing in with LinkedIn on the application page is an identity check, not a persistent account. LinkedIn's authorization server sends the confirmation back to a short-lived, server-side function, which reads your name, email, and photo from the token and stores them in a signed, HttpOnly cookie for a few minutes — just long enough to pre-fill the application form. We don't create a login session, and we don't use this to post to LinkedIn on your behalf or access your connections.

5. Sharing of information

We don't sell or rent your information. We don't share application or recommendation details with third parties except:

  • The service providers that host the site and process form submissions on our behalf.
  • LinkedIn, to the extent required to complete the sign-in flow described above.
  • Publicly, if you're accepted and featured — and only the interview content you review and approve before it goes live.

6. Cookies & local storage

ProductMoat doesn't run third-party analytics or advertising trackers. The only cookie we set is the short-lived LinkedIn verification cookie described above, which expires automatically a few minutes after it's created. We also use your browser's local storage to remember your theme preference — this stays on your device and is never sent to us.

7. Data retention

We keep application and recommendation details for as long as needed to review them and, if you're featured, to keep your published profile accurate. If you'd like your application or recommendation data deleted, contact us and we'll remove it.

8. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it — including withdrawing a published interview. Reach out using the contact details below and we'll handle it directly.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us, including keeping the LinkedIn verification exchange server-side and never exposing credentials or tokens to the browser. No method of transmission or storage is ever fully secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's privacy

ProductMoat is intended for working professionals and isn't directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves. If we make a material change, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
